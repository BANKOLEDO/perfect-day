# 📅 Daily Planner

A beautiful, interactive daily planner with drag-and-drop functionality. Plan your perfect 24-hour day with ease!


##  Features

-  **Drag & Drop Interface** - Intuitive activity placement
-  **24-Hour Visual Timeline** - See your entire day at a glance
-  **Customizable Activities** - Create and edit activities
-  **Real-time Statistics** - Track hours used and efficiency
-  **Quick Templates** - Productive, Balanced, and Creative schedules
-  **Auto-save** - Your progress is automatically saved
-  **Responsive Design** - Works on all devices
-  **Beautiful Animations** - Smooth transitions and interactions

##  Quick Start

1. **Clone or download** the project files
2. **Open `index.html`** in your web browser
3. **Start planning!** Drag activities onto the timeline

##  How to Use

### Adding Activities
1. **Drag** any activity from the left palette
2. **Drop** it on an empty time slot in the timeline
3. **Activities automatically span** their duration (1-8 hours)

### Managing Your Day
- **Click on filled slots** to remove activities
- **Use templates** for quick schedule setup
- **Create custom activities** with the "Add" button
- **Edit default activities** with the edit button (pencil icon)

### Saving & Exporting
- **Auto-save**: Your timeline is automatically saved
- **Manual save**: Use "Save Plan" to confirm your schedule
- **Export**: Download your schedule as a text file

##  Customization

### Creating Custom Activities
1. Click the **"Add"** button
2. Enter activity **name** and **duration**
3. Choose a **color theme**
4. Click **"Create Activity"**

### Editing Activities
1. Click the **edit button** (pencil icon)
2. Modify **name**, **duration**, or **icon**
3. Save changes

### Color Themes
- Blue: Work & Focus
- Green: Health & Exercise
- Yellow: Learning
- Purple: Creativity
- Pink: Social & Fun
- Orange: Meals & Breaks
- Gray: Rest & Relaxation

## Understanding Statistics

- **Hours Used**: Total hours with scheduled activities
- **Hours Free**: Remaining unscheduled hours
- **Activities**: Number of different activity types used
- **Efficiency**: Percentage of day utilized

##  Troubleshooting

### Activities Not Dragging?
- Ensure JavaScript is enabled
- Try a different browser if issues persist

### Timeline Not Loading?
- Clear browser cache and reload
- Check browser console for errors

### Lost Your Data?
- Data is stored in browser localStorage
- Clearing browser data will reset the planner
